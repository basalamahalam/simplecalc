# Privacy Policy for SimpleCalc

**Last updated: 2026-11-24**

SimpleCalc is a simple calculator app. This policy explains, plainly, what happens to your data when you use it.

## Data collection

SimpleCalc does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or device data of any kind. There is:

- No internet access — the app has no network permissions and never sends data anywhere.
- No analytics or tracking of any kind.
- No advertising.
- No account, login, or sign-up.
- No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage, or any other device permission.

## How calculations work

Every calculation happens entirely on your device, in memory, while the app is open. Nothing you type is saved, logged, or sent anywhere — closing the app discards it completely.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that uses data), this document will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
